(02)QuestionsShort answers
Where's my key?

It arrives right after checkout. Open clipped, paste it on the welcome screen, done. Keys are long on purpose: they're signed by evzero, so they can't be guessed or made up.

Does clipped phone home to check my key?

No. The key is checked on your computer, offline. clipped only ever talks to Twitch, YouTube, Instagram and the AI you choose.

What happens when a monthly key runs out?

clipped asks for a key again. Your settings, games and review queue are all still there when you paste the next one.

Can I switch from monthly to lifetime?

Any time: get a lifetime key, paste it in, and cancel the monthly plan.

What do I need besides the key?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and free Twitch developer keys (about 3 minutes, the app shows you how). YouTube and Instagram are optional: without them you download each Short and post it yourself.
