(02)QuestionsShort answers
Where's my Pro key?

It appears right here after checkout, and this browser remembers it. Lost it? Use “Find my key” at the bottom of this page with the subscription id from Razorpay's email (it starts with sub_) and the email you paid with.

Can I use Pro on two computers?

One at a time. To switch, sign out in Settings → Licence on the old one, or pick “Move Pro to this computer” on the new one (up to 3 moves a month). That's what keeps one subscription from being shared around.

Does clipped need the internet to check my key?

Pro checks in with evzero.org about once a day and keeps working for a week offline. Free day keys are checked on your computer. Only the key and an anonymous id for your computer are sent.

How do I cancel?

Right here: Cancel Pro below, with the subscription id from Razorpay’s email and the email you paid with. Or email r@evzero.org. Payments stop straight away and Pro keeps working until the end of the month you paid for. See the refund policy.

What do I need besides a key?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and free Twitch developer keys (about 3 minutes; the app shows you how). YouTube and Instagram are optional: without them you download each Short and post it yourself.
