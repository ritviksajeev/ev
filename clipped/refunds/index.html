Cancellation & refunds

Last updated 19 November 2026

Cancelling Pro

Cancel any time with “Cancel Pro” at the bottom of the plans page (you need the subscription id from Razorpay’s email and the email you paid with), or by emailing r@evzero.org. Cancelling stops future payments; Pro keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for.

Refunds

If clipped doesn't work for you, email us within 7 days of a payment and we'll refund that payment. Otherwise payments for months already started aren't refunded, except where the law requires it. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 business days.

Delivery

clipped is software, delivered digitally. Your Pro key appears on screen right after checkout (and can be found again with “Find my key” on the plans page); the app is a download from evzero.org. Nothing is shipped.

Free days

Free day keys cost nothing and aren't refundable or transferable.
